About the researcher

I'm a political researcher and writer interested in elections, democratic institutions, Southern politics, and political communication. My work combines qualitative research with data analysis to examine not only what happens in politics, but the systems and messages behind it.

Areas of interest

  • Election administration and electoral competition
  • Political communication and campaign messaging
  • Voting rights and democratic institutions
  • Southern and state politics
